This project develops **natural language question answering over the Bhāvaprakāśanighaṇṭu (BPN) knowledge graph**, extending [Saṅgrāhaka](/projects/sangrahaka/), our platform for annotating and querying knowledge graphs.

The goal is to let users ask questions in natural language and obtain answers grounded in the annotated Sanskrit and Ayurveda knowledge graph. The work connects natural language questions to graph queries while preserving domain-specific terminology such as *Rasa*, *Guṇa*, *Vīrya*, and *Vipāka*.

The project explores how knowledge graph grounding can support faithful, traceable answers from classical Sanskrit sources.
