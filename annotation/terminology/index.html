Before you begin

This study evaluates Sanskrit-based technical terminology. For each concept, you will see an English definition and a list of proposed Sanskrit terms.

  1. Read the definition and indicate how familiar you are with the concept.
  2. Rate every proposed term for Accuracy and Understandability, using the definitions below. Rate similar spellings separately.
  3. Use Cannot judge for either rating if you cannot assess it. You can give one rating and choose Cannot judge for the other.
  4. Select Save and next after completing the ratings for a concept. You can take breaks and resume later.

Accuracy: How well does the term express the concept in the definition?

Understandability: How easy would the term be for a learner with basic Sanskrit knowledge to understand when introduced with this definition?

Use 1 (very poor), 2 (poor), 3 (moderate), 4 (good), or 5 (very good). All terms for a concept may receive low scores, high scores, or the same score. Rate each term separately; you do not need to rank the terms or use every score on the scale.

We ask you to complete 100 concepts. Further concepts are optional. This task is unpaid and voluntary; you may stop at any time. We estimate 5–10 hours for 100 concepts, spread across several sessions.

Acknowledgment: Participants who complete ratings for at least 100 concepts will be eligible for acknowledgment in resulting publications or project materials, with their permission. Contributions below this threshold may not qualify for acknowledgment.

We will use your ratings for research and may report them in publications under a participant ID. Your name and email will not be published with your ratings. For questions or to request withdrawal, contact the organizer through the group website.
