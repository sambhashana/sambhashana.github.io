Before you begin

About this study

You will see a concept definition and distinct proposed terms. Rate each term independently for Accuracy and Understandability. Everyone receives the same two questions, and you may choose “Cannot judge” whenever needed.

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time. Your coded ratings may be analysed and reported in research publications; your name and email will not be published with them. Contact the organizer through the group website with questions or to request withdrawal.

Each concept has several distinct terms to rate. You can take breaks and resume later. The organizer will provide the task-specific time estimate and compensation information before you participate.

Accuracy: How well does the term express the concept in the definition? Understandability: How easy would it be for a learner with basic Sanskrit knowledge to understand the term when introduced with this definition? Rate each from 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good), or choose “Cannot judge”.

Instructions and rating guide

1 = very poor · 2 = poor · 3 = moderate · 4 = good · 5 = very good. These are perceived judgments with the definition visible, not a test of unaided understanding.
