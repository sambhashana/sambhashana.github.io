Hrishikesh Terdalkar, as a member of the BharatGen consortium, attended the **BharatGen Consortium Workshop**, held at **IIT Bombay** on **14–15 October 2026**. The event celebrated **two years of BharatGen** and was graced by the presence of **S. Krishnan, Secretary, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)**.
Hrishikesh gave a talk titled **“Role of Indian Linguistic Theories in AI”**.
