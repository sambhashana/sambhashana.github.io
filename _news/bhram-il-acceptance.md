The paper [BHRAM-IL: A Benchmark for Hallucination Recognition and Assessment in Multiple Indian Languages](https://aclanthology.org/2025.bhasha-1.9/) by **Hrishikesh Terdalkar, Kirtan Bhojani, Aryan Dongare, and Omm Aditya Behera** was accepted as a full paper at the BHASHA 2025 workshop, co-located with IJCNLP–AACL 2025, IIT Bombay.
