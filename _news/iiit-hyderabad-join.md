Hrishikesh Terdalkar will be joining the [International Institute of Information Technology, Hyderabad (IIIT-H)](https://iiit.ac.in) as Assistant Professor at the [Language Technologies Research Center (LTRC)](https://ltrc.iiit.ac.in), effective 4 August 2026. The Saṁbhāṣaṇa Research Group will continue at the new institution.
