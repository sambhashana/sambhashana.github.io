Hrishikesh Terdalkar was invited as a panelist at the **SummerSaaS AI Hackathon 2026**, hosted at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus on 17–18 May 2026. The event, backed by Google, TASK, and THub, brought together 600+ student participants and 300+ teams from 100+ colleges across India.

He participated in **Panel Discussion 2: Navigating Careers in Today's World – Insights from Industry Experts**, alongside Aarshad Devani (Lead, Google Developer Groups Hyderabad) and Prashanth (Senior Manager, Deloitte USI), moderated by Vamshi Guguloth. The discussion covered career trajectories, the evolving role of AI across industries, skills for the future, and advice for students entering the workforce.
